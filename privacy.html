Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Individual Entrepreneur Denis Ivankov ("Denis Ivankov, IE", "we", "us") handles information in the games and applications we publish on Steam, Google Play and other platforms (the "Apps"). It applies to every App that links to this page.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account with us, and we do not collect your name, address, contacts, photos or precise location.

Depending on the App, the following limited information may be processed:

  • Platform account identifiers. For online features (for example multiplayer lobbies and inviting friends), the App uses your platform identity, such as your Steam ID and display name, or your Google Play Games profile. This information is provided by the platform and is used only to run those features.
  • Game data. Settings, progress, scores and leaderboard entries may be stored on your device or through the platform's cloud services.
  • Technical and diagnostic data. Crash reports and basic device information (device model, operating system version, App version) may be collected to find and fix errors.
  • Network data. In online multiplayer, game data is exchanged between players' devices, either directly or through the platform's relay servers. Your IP address may be visible to the platform's networking service as part of this process.

2. How we use information

  • to provide the App's features, including online play, saving progress and leaderboards;
  • to diagnose problems and improve stability and performance;
  • to respond to your messages when you contact us.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

3. Third-party services

The Apps may rely on the following services, which process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Valve Corporation (Steam) — Steam Privacy Policy
  • Google (Google Play, Google Play Games services) — Google Privacy Policy
  • Unity Technologies (game engine and related services) — Unity Privacy Policy

If a specific App uses additional services, such as analytics or advertising, they are listed on that App's store page in the data safety section, and this page will be updated accordingly.

4. Data retention

Data stored on your device remains there until you delete the App or its data. Diagnostic data is kept only as long as needed to fix the related issue. Data held by platforms (Steam, Google) is retained according to their policies.

5. Children

The Apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information about you, or to object to its processing. To make a request, contact us at the address below. Data held by Steam or Google can also be managed through your account settings on those platforms.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version is always available on this page, with the effective date shown at the top.
